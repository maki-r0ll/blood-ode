Welcome to Blood Ode

Etharis is a dark and fractured world. The gods have vanished, leaving only silence in their wake. In their stead, demons and angels stride openly across the land, shaping mortal destiny with fire and fury. Mortals endure amidst the ruins of forgotten ages, where ancient ley lines—once lifeblood to gods and men alike—now lie unstable and contested, their power coveted by all.

Time itself is marked by blood moons, each crimson rise casting its light every fifth week upon a people who know only that their world is fragile, fading, and perilous. From dreadwights who linger as twisted echoes of their former selves, to dragon-kin who walk as both human and wyrm, every shadowed corner of Etharis whispers of survival in the absence of divine order.
